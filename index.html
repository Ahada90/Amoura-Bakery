About Amoura's Bakery

Crafting delicious, homemade cakes and pastries with love right from our kitchen in Majengo, Mombasa.

Home About Us

✦ Homemade With Love ✦

Baked Fresh Daily

At Amoura's Bakery, we believe that the best sweet treats are made with warmth, premium ingredients, and genuine care. What started as a home baking passion in Majengo, Mombasa has grown into a beloved local brand serving custom cakes, churros, dessert tubs, and savory bites across the city.

As a 100% home-based bakery, every order is handcrafted specifically for you. Whether you are celebrating a birthday, hosting an event, or simply craving a sweet delight, we bake each item fresh to ensure maximum quality and flavor.
